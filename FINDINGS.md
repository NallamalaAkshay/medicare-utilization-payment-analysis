# Findings — 2024 Medicare Utilization and Payment

## Service volume

Clinical Laboratory had the highest total service volume nationally in the specialty summary: 304,353,832.8 service units. Service units are not a count of patients.

## Comparable office-visit payments

For HCPCS 99213, Internal Medicine, and office place of service, 38,403 provider-service rows contributed to the state comparison. Among the 16 states or areas with at least 100,000 service units, the median of the state-level average Medicare payments was $58.52 per service. New York had the highest average at $69.93, and Ohio had the lowest at $49.84.

## Statistical summary

The 25th and 75th percentiles of state-level average payment were $55.88 and $60.83. Spearman's correlation between state service volume and average payment was 0.718 in this selected group. This is a descriptive association; it does not show that volume causes payment differences.

## Practical takeaway

The difference between New York and Ohio suggests a useful next question: how much of the variation is associated with geographic payment rules or provider characteristics? This analysis identifies where to investigate, but cannot determine the cause on its own.

## Limitations

The payment comparison holds HCPCS code, specialty, and place of service constant, but does not adjust for every factor affecting payment. State refers to the provider's location, not necessarily the patient's residence. The 100,000-service threshold excludes smaller states or areas from the statistical summary. Published CMS data and these checks do not establish that every record is error-free.
